Care guide

Original P1 rules, sped up. 400ms real time is one game minute.

Buttons

  • A cycles Food → Lights → Game → Medicine → Toilet → Status → Discipline
  • B opens or confirms
  • C backs out. Game: A left, C right
  • Keys: A / Left, S Space Enter, D / Right / Esc

Keep them alive

  • Meals fill hunger (+1g, 4/day). Snacks add hunger and happy (+2g); 4+ snacks make them sick
  • Night is 21:00–08:00. Lights off to sleep. Lights on at night is a care mistake. Lights off in the day and they call
  • Flush poop after meals. Two piles → sick. Medicine only works with the skull
  • Win 3 of 5 left/right rounds for +2 happy and −1g
  • Scold only when they call with full meters (naughty)

Life

  • Egg 48m → baby 7h → child 11h → teen 14h → adult. Old age around 6–8 days
  • Baby Mary has no bow yet
  • Progress saves in this browser. Tab hidden pauses time
