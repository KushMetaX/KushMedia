Privacy Policy

Effective: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KushMetaX collects, uses, and shares personal information through KushMetaX websites and tools (“Services”).

What we collect

  • Email and salted password hashes for KushMetaX accounts.
  • Cookies/session identifiers used to keep you logged in securely.
  • Technical diagnostics (approximate IP/country telemetry, timestamps, quotas, error logs) to run and secure the Services.
  • Content you voluntarily submit inside features (such as DogiDex entries keyed to your account).

Payments (crypto processor)

For Doginal Dogs Plus, our payment processor collects payment-related information needed to generate addresses and confirm transactions. Their privacy notice applies to that processing. KushMetaX receives limited data (e.g. payment ids, statuses, amounts, and the email you provide at checkout) to activate Plus features.

How we use information

To authenticate you, meter rate limits/quota, operate Dex sync storage, troubleshoot outages, deter abuse/fraud proportional to KushMetaX’s role, comply with lawful requests narrowly scoped.

Sharing

We use hosting/logging providers incidental to SaaS delivery. Our payment processor handles the crypto checkout layer per its notices. Except as required lawfully/KushMetaX contractually necessary, we do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep account rows while your account remains or where reasonable backups/legal holds require.

Your choices

Depending on geography you may exercise access/erasure/objection/portability requests through contact channels referenced on Connect; fulfillment may require validating account ownership safely.

Children

Not directed at individuals under contractual age thresholds; do not sign up children.
