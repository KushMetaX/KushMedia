Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KushMetaX collects, uses, and shares personal information through KushMetaX websites and tools (“Services”).

What we collect

  • Email and salted password hashes for KushMetaX accounts.
  • Cookies/session identifiers used to keep you logged in securely.
  • Technical diagnostics (approximate IP/country telemetry, timestamps, quotas, error logs) to run and secure the Services.
  • Content you voluntarily submit inside features (such as DogiDex entries keyed to your account).

How we use information

To authenticate you, apply rate limits, operate DogiDex sync, troubleshoot outages, deter abuse, and comply with lawful requests.

Sub-processors

These services process data when you use the corresponding feature:

  • Supabase — account authentication and DogiDex row storage (project host tblktaifczarnesqucwz.supabase.co).
  • Mapbox — map tiles and geocoding for the invite-only Voyager map.
  • Google Fonts — typefaces loaded in the browser.
  • jsDelivr — only if a page still loads a pinned library from that CDN. The DDL checklist PDF tools are self-hosted when that build is deployed.
  • Discord CDN (cdn.discordapp.com, media.discordapp.net) — avatar images for Discord-linked accounts.
  • Solana RPC (solana-rpc.publicnode.com, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com) and Arbitrum RPC (arb1.arbitrum.io) — Oracle Madness price reads. Your browser talks to those endpoints directly.

KushMetaX does not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep account rows while your account remains or where reasonable backups/legal holds require.

Your choices
