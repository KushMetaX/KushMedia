Refund & cancellation

Effective: April 29, 2026

Payment processor. Doginal Dogs Plus is sold as prepaid access through a cryptocurrency payment provider (e.g. NOWPayments). That provider handles wallet addresses, on-chain settlement, receipts, and any refund or dispute mechanics under its own policies.

Renewals. Access is time-limited; extend Plus by paying again from the DD Evaluator before your prepaid period ends (there is no card-on-file auto-charge).

KushMetaX entitlement. Plus unlock updates when our server receives a verified “finished” (or equivalent) status from the provider. Use the same email as your KushMetaX account so entitlements attach correctly.

Refund questions. Start with the receipt and support flow from your payment provider; reversals follow their rules and on-chain realities.
